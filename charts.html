Charts & data viz

Ink makes the point. Gray carries the rest. Body blue is the accent series. Teal and red are semantic only. Never a cobalt prism series.

Chart palette (ordered)

The hero series — the point of the chart — is ink (#262626) or body blue (#2D6FA8). Supporting series walk the gray ramp. Teal / red only when the data itself is success / failure.

Semantic extras, off the default cycle: teal #00B1A6 (success), red #F5563F (negative). Yellow and sky are chrome — do not cycle them as series colors; they will look like decoration.

Chart rules

  • One hero series per chart. If you cannot say which series is the point, none of them gets ink or blue — all gray.
  • No purple, no cobalt prism, no rainbow.
  • Do not use yellow or sky as data series unless you are literally charting those brand tokens. They read as chrome, not as data.
  • No 3D charts. Ever.
  • No chart borders or drop shadows on the plot. The frame, if any, is the product color-frame around a screenshot of a chart — not a box around axes.
  • Label directly. Legends are a last resort.
  • Grid: Line #E0E0E0, 0.5px, horizontal only.
  • Axes: Muted #6B6B6B, 1px. Only show axes that carry information.
  • Font: Geist 12px for labels; Geist Mono / JetBrains Mono 11px for ticks and values. Never bold in chart text.
  • Bar width: wider than the gaps. Minimum 60% fill ratio.
  • Pie charts: avoid. Horizontal bars instead. Maximum 4 slices if you must.

Python / matplotlib

Set these defaults at the top of your notebook or script.
