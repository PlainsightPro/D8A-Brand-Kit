How color is used on the site

  • Page is canvas. Cards are white. Headings and buttons are ink.
  • Body copy is body blue, never muted gray (too low contrast) and never sky (too light for text).
  • Primary CTA is ink fill, white type, uppercase. Outline buttons are ink stroke on canvas.
  • Window chrome is always three dots: red, yellow, sky — in that order.
  • Product frames are hard-stop gradients of red / yellow / sky-deep. See Graphics.
  • Ambient orbs (sky, teal, yellow, red) sit behind the hero at low opacity. Atmosphere, not a statement.
  • Bronze / silver / gold only for medallion (Bronze–Silver–Gold) chips. Not a palette for charts or UI.

Banned

Do
Canvas page, ink heading, body-blue paragraph, one color-framed screenshot

The triad frames the product. The type stays readable.

Don’t
Cobalt / indigo / pink gradients, pearl #fbfaff, Inter-on-violet, gradient headlines

That is v4.0 Prism and generic AI-saas. D8A is not that.

Accessibility

  • Ink on canvas: ~12.6:1 — WCAG AAA
  • Body blue on canvas: designed ≥ 4.5:1 — WCAG AA body text. This is why it is #2D6FA8, not sky.
  • Muted on canvas: ~4.6:1 — AA for secondary text
  • White on ink: AAA
  • Dark-sub on ink: use for secondary lines on dark, not for long body
  • Sky, yellow, red, teal are not body-text colors. Large UI and decoration only, unless contrast is re-checked.
