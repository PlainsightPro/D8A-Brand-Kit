Common rules

  • Never use more than two weights on a single surface (e.g. Regular + Bold).
  • Headings are Geist 700. Do not use 800/900 — Geist on the site stops at 700.
  • Body line-height is 1.55–1.7. Headings are 1.1–1.3.
  • Negative letter-spacing on headings only (28px and above).
  • Mono never sets headings or body copy. Labels, stats, values, captions, URLs, and code only.
  • Buttons: uppercase, tracking, Geist 600. Not title case, not all-small.
  • Sentence case for headings. The wordmark D8A is the exception.

CSS font stacks

--font-sans: "Geist", "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif; --font-display: "Geist", "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif; --font-mono: "Geist Mono", "JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, monospace;
