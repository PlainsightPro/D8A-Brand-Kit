Graphic elements

Four gestures from the live site: the infinity stroke, window chrome, color frames, and soft ambient orbs. The cobalt prism pulse is retired.

Infinity stroke

The primary graphic mark. Stroke only, never filled. Same path as the logo. On the site it sits next to the wordmark; at large scale and low opacity it can watermark a hero.

Ink on light
White on ink
  • Never fill the infinity. The openness is the loop.
  • Never draw it in the triad colors at logo scale. Chrome colors belong to dots and frames.
  • Hero watermark: large, ~8% opacity, ink on canvas.
  • No prism pulse traveling the path. That was v4.0.

Window chrome

Three dots — red, yellow, sky, always in that order — plus a mono URL. This is how every product mock on the site is introduced. Also used as “hero-dots” above headlines.

Color frames

Hard-stop gradients around product shots. Four sanctioned recipes from the site. Padding ~10px, radius 22px. The mock sits inside.

frame-a · yellow → sky-deep · 115°

frame-b · red → sky-deep → yellow · 245°

frame-c · red → yellow → sky-deep · 205°

frame-d · sky-deep → yellow · 115°

  • One frame per composition.
  • Hard stops, not soft blends. The site uses percentage stops, not a wash.
  • No indigo, no pink, no cobalt.

Ambient orbs

Large, heavily blurred circles in sky / teal / yellow / red, drifting slowly behind the page. Atmosphere only. They must never compete with type. Disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

Numbered badges

White pill, hairline rule, Geist Mono. Used as section indexes: 01, 02, 03, Benefits, Concepts.

Vocabulary chips

The site uses a field of terms (Context lake, Specs are the new code, Technology agnostic, Data Mesh…). Hairline pills, some tinted with sky or teal. Do not treat this as a random tag cloud of buzzwords — only terms D8A actually uses.

Do / Don’t

Do
Infinity stroke, chrome dots, one color frame, soft orbs, numbered mono badges
Don’t
Prism pulse on the loop, gradient underlines, glassmorphism stacks, emoji, decorative 3D
