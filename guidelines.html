How to use this kit

The rules that apply to every D8A deliverable. If you are creating anything with our name on it, start here. When this kit and another document disagree, this kit and d8a.pro win.

The seven rules

01

Ship it clean

Every deliverable uses the correct logo, colors, and fonts. No exceptions. If the template exists, use it. Match d8a.pro, not an older deck.

02

No fluff

If a sentence does not say anything, delete it. D8A copy is direct, specific, and useful. Buzzwords are banned. Use the lines in Voice.

03

Light canvas, soft depth

The page is #F7F7F7. Cards are white. Depth is a 2–10px soft shadow, not gloss, bevels, or hard drop-shadows. Hover motion is 150–300ms and 1px of lift.

04

No AI purple

Never use indigo/violet/pink gradients. The retired Prism cobalt (#5340e6, #2e6bff) is off-brand. Color lives in the triad (sky, yellow, red, teal) as chrome and frames, and in body blue for copy.

05

Ink first, color last

Design in ink on canvas first. If it does not work in black and white, it does not work. Then add window chrome, one color-frame, or body-blue copy. The triad is atmosphere around the product, not a rainbow theme.

06

Prove it with the product

Do not claim — show. Prefer real app.d8a.pro screenshots in a color frame. Do not invent customer names, volumes, or case studies that are not in this kit.

07

Accessible and consistent

Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (that is why body is #2D6FA8, not sky). SVG icons, not emoji. cursor-pointer on controls. Visible :focus-visible. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Pre-send checklist

Logo is infinity + D8A, correct variant for this background?
Headings and body in Geist; labels, stats, and code in Geist Mono / JetBrains Mono?
Canvas #F7F7F7, ink #262626, body #2D6FA8 — no purple, no pearl, no cobalt prism?
Copy is a line we actually say on d8a.pro, or it could sit next to those lines?
Product proof is a real screenshot, color-framed, with window chrome?
No emoji-as-icons, no layout-shifting hovers, focus rings present?
Every claim is backed by this kit, the site, or the product — not invented?

When in doubt

Open this brand kit, then open d8a.pro. If what you are making does not look like it belongs next to those pages, revisit. The live site is the canonical visual; this kit is the written spec.
