Iconography

Stroke SVG, currentColor, no emoji. The marketing site uses custom 24×24 icons in the same family as Lucide / Heroicons: 1.5–2.5px stroke, round caps, optional 8–14% fill for depth.

Style rules

  • SVG only. No emoji as icons. Hard rule from the site checklist.
  • Stroke 1.5–2.5px on a 24px canvas. Round linecaps and joins.
  • Color is currentColor. Icons inherit the text they sit with — ink, not sky, unless they are a single accent.
  • Optional hairline fill at 8–14% opacity for depth (the benefit icons). Do not switch to solid filled Material icons.
  • Size: 16 / 20 / 24 / 28px. 16 inline, 20 UI, 24 nav, 28 benefit cards.
  • 8px between an icon and its label.
  • Platform logos (Databricks, Snowflake, Fabric…) are the vendor SVGs, not redraws. Keep vendor color in a small well; do not recolor them to the triad.

Don’t

Do
One stroke family, currentColor, consistent sizes, SVG from Lucide/Heroicons or the site set
Don’t
Mix Font Awesome + Material + emoji, put icons in colored circles, or gradient an icon
