What changed in v5.0

v4.0 Prism (cobalt gradient on pearl, Inter, AI-adjacent purple) is retired. v5.0 is the identity that ships on d8a.pro — the Bauplan-inspired light canvas from d8a-website-v2.

  • No AI purple. The cobalt prism (#5340e6 → #2e6bff) is gone. Accents are sky, yellow, red, and teal — used as chrome and frames, not as a gradient theme.
  • Light canvas + ink. Page background is #F7F7F7. Type and primary buttons are ink #262626. Body copy is blue #2D6FA8 (WCAG AA).
  • Geist, not Inter. UI, headings, and body are Geist. Labels and code are Geist Mono / JetBrains Mono. See Fonts.
  • Infinity + wordmark. The logo is a stroke infinity lockup plus D8A, all ink. The 8 is not a gradient. See Logos.
  • Color-frame product mocks. Product screenshots sit in red / yellow / sky frames with window chrome. The product is the proof. See Photography and Graphics.
  • Copy from the live site. Headlines, benefits, cascade, and FAQ in this kit match d8a-website-v2. See Positioning and Voice.
