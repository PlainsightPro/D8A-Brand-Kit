Logo

The lockup from d8a.pro: a stroke infinity plus the D8A wordmark, both in currentColor. Ink on light. White on ink. Never a gradient 8.

Primary lockup

This is the mark on the marketing site, the app chrome, and this kit. The infinity is the symbol of the loop (request → spec → code → ship). The letters are Geist 700, tracking -0.03em.

App icon & avatar

For favicons, app icons, and social avatars, the infinity sits alone on a rounded square. Ink on canvas, or white on ink. Do not put a gradient 8 in the square — that was v4.0 Prism and is retired.

Clear space

The minimum clear space around the lockup is equal to the height of the infinity mark. No other element may intrude into this zone.

D8A

The dashed border represents the minimum clear-space boundary.

Minimum size

  • Digital lockup: 24px height minimum
  • Print lockup: 10mm height minimum
  • Infinity only: 16px / 6mm minimum

Don’t

  • Don’t rotate, skew, or distort the mark
  • Don’t add drop shadows, outlines, or bevels
  • Don’t set the wordmark in a font other than Geist
  • Don’t fill the infinity
  • Don’t gradient the 8, the D, the A, or the loop — that was Prism, it is retired
  • Don’t recolor the lockup with sky, yellow, red, or teal; those colors belong to chrome and frames
  • Don’t lock up the wordmark with other brand names
