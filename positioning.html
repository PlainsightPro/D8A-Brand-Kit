Value proposition

The autonomous D8A platform. Every person is a data person.

D8A is an AI-powered DataOps workspace that manages the entire data engineering lifecycle — from business strategy and data requests through specifications, code generation, review, and merge — in one platform, on your stack. Technology-agnostic. Context-driven. AI-generated, human-curated.

The cascade

The product story is one cascade, not a feature list. Keep this order when you explain D8A.

01 · Business first

Strategy → Portfolio → Discovery

D8A starts from your business strategy. One cascade — business strategy, data portfolio, and missing insights — locked to what your company is trying to win.

02 · Specs

Request → Spec → Code

Specs are the new code. The durable asset is the specification. Plain-language requests become living specs. Code is generated from them and cannot drift.

03 · Architecture

Blueprint → Marketplace → Canvas

Architecture drives the code. Define layers, patterns, and tech once in the Blueprint — or pick a curated architecture from the Marketplace. Generated code follows your playbook.

04 · Command

In the loop · On the loop · Autonomous

Human in command. Always. AI codes, reviews, and merges at the automation level you choose, per step, per work item.

What D8A is — and what it isn’t

What we are

  • An AI-powered DataOps workspace
  • Platform-agnostic: Databricks, Snowflake, Fabric, PostgreSQL, Azure SQL, Redshift, MySQL, SQL Server, Oracle, Synapse — via pyodbc / ODBC
  • Spec-first: the spec is the durable asset, code is generated from it
  • Business-led: strategy locks the portfolio and the use cases
  • Human in command: autonomy is a dial you set

What we are not

  • A replacement for your engineers
  • An autocomplete (Copilot / Cursor) for SQL
  • Your next data platform technology
  • A vendor lock-in — specs and generated code live in your Git
  • A BI tool or an ETL platform

Ideal customer profile

Audience on the live site: data leaders, platform teams, requestors.

Buyer

Head of Data / data leader

Owns strategy, delivery, and platform decisions. Tired of process tax, handoffs, and “who owns this?” Wants the team shipping work that moves the business.

Users

Platform / data engineer

Builds on the stack you already run. Wants architecture encoded once, specs that don’t drift, and AI that follows the playbook instead of inventing a new one.

Requestor / business user

Describes what they need in plain language. Does not care about infrastructure. Every person is a data person.

Data product owner

Bridges strategy and delivery. Needs a scored portfolio, living specs, and a board that shows Specifying → Coding → Reviewing → Merging.

Customer pains

01

Days lost to handoffs

The wait between a business question and a production answer is process tax: rewrites, status, and “who owns this?”

02

Senior time on tickets

Best people burn capacity on status, tool-switching, and boilerplate instead of decisions that change the numbers.

03

Loudest-request roadmaps

Low-value work gets funded. Strategy, budget, and impact are not aligned.

04

Vague requests, drifting code

Business language never becomes a reviewable spec. Code drifts from what was asked. Ticket ping-pong.

05

Architecture rotting in a wiki

Patterns and standards are documentation, not instructions the AI follows. Every pipeline is a new invention.

06

2018 workflow in an AI era

LLMs can write code. The data team still uses the same process from 2018.

07

Autocomplete is not a team member

Copilot / Cursor help one developer type faster. They do not manage the lifecycle from request to merge against your architecture.

08

Platform lock-in

Every vendor wants to be the entire stack. Switching costs grow. Specs should stay put when the target changes.

Facts you can claim (from the FAQ)

  • The AI drafts specifications, generates code from your patterns, reviews, and merges. Your team curates the context, sets the automation level, and stays in command. It removes process work, not engineers.
  • D8A works on your metadata — schemas, lineage, specifications, and code — not on your row-level data. Workspaces are Git-backed and role-gated, with a full audit trail.
  • The core platform is not open source today. The specification format and generated code live in your own Git repositories — readable, portable, and yours.
  • Self-hosting is on the roadmap for enterprise plans. Early access runs as a managed workspace.
  • Do not invent volumes, customer names, or case studies that are not in this kit or in the product.
