Templates

Starting points for the documents we make most often. Every template should look like a page of d8a.pro moved onto another format.

Available templates

Presentation

Sales deck

16:9. Canvas background, ink type, Geist. One color-framed product shot per story slide. Chrome dots on the title. CTA is an ink pill, not a gradient.

Document

One-pager

Single-page PDF/Word for product overviews. Headline from the site. Cascade in four numbered badges. Screenshot in a color frame.

Document

Technical whitepaper

Multi-page. Geist body, JetBrains Mono for specs and SQL. Ink headings. No purple callout boxes.

Email

Email signature

Infinity mark + D8A wordmark, name, title, hello@d8a.pro. Ink on white. No slogan stack under the name.

Social

LinkedIn carousel

1080×1350. Title slide with chrome dots + site headline. Content slides: one idea, product proof. Close: “Stop managing process. Start shipping data.”

Social

Social image / OG

1200×630. Canvas, wordmark, one headline, optional framed screenshot. Soft orbs allowed at low opacity. No prism gradient.

What every template inherits from the site

  • Headline voice: sentence case, Geist 700, tight tracking. Use a live-site line when one exists.
  • Eyebrows: Geist Mono, uppercase, letter-spacing, body-blue or muted.
  • Primary button: ink fill, white type, uppercase tracking, 150–250ms hover lift.
  • Product proof: mock window (red / yellow / sky dots + mono URL) inside a color frame.
  • Footer: infinity + D8A, d8a.pro, hello@d8a.pro.

Template rules

  • Always start from the template — never from a blank document that still has Inter / cobalt / pearl in it.
  • Do not change fonts, colors, or layout structure.
  • Content can be added or removed, but the visual system stays.
  • If you need a template that does not exist, request it — do not improvise a purple slide.
